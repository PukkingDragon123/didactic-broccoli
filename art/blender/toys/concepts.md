# Gacha Garage: the Blender toy line (six series)

Six original capsule-toy series, in the spirit of the owner's gashapon boards (ref_gacha 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11). Each one
has a hook, a twist and a gimmick you can play with in the game. All names, characters and logos are our own. They
borrow no product, character or brand from the boards: the boards show the genre (transforming cans, hooded animals,
sniper sparrows, shadow monsters, gods as birds), and each series below takes a genre and does something new with it.

Every figure is its own sculpt (no reskins). Finish across the line: factory PVC with airbrushed gradients, crisp
paint lines, printed details and bead eyes with a catchlight, as a 512 px pixel-art paint over a sculpt baked onto a
low-poly game mesh (≤1.5k tris), with a normal map. Sizes are 40 to 65 mm. Each series has five regular figures and a
secret.

## 1. ヘンシン文具 Henshin Bungu: "Stationery Beasts" (transformation)

**Hook:** Shōwa school stationery that unfolds into the animal it always secretly was. 完全変形! Each one is a
believable desk object in one state and a creature with a face in the other.
**Twist:** the object's own mechanism is the creature's anatomy: the stapler's hinge is the crocodile's jaw, the
sharpener's crank is the pig's curly tail, the binder clip's levers are the stag beetle's jaws, the protractor is the
crane's wings.
**Gimmick:** press the button to play the transformation. The creature folds up into the object, holds there, then
springs back out with its signature move (a chomp, a snap, a wing fan). It plays in your hand, on the shelf and while
you inspect it.

## 2. なかのひと Naka no Hito: "Who's in the Suit?" (costume and disguise)

**Hook:** Shōwa tokusatsu kaijū suits on set, each worn by a very small animal actor.
**Twist:** the suit gives it away: a zip down the back, sweat, a little face peeping out of the monster's mouth, a
towel round its neck between takes.
**Gimmick:** press the button and the kaijū head tips back on its hinge to show the actor inside (a hamster, a
quail, a shiba pup, a newt, a kitten); press again and it is back in character. Every suit is its own original
monster: a drill-horned mole beast, a smokestack lizard, a radio-tower moth, a clam robot, a rice-paddy hydra.
Secret: the director, a tortoise in a sun visor, wearing the one suit that has no one inside.

## 3. はしご乗り Hashigo Nori: "New Year Ladder Frogs" (playset with action)

**Hook:** Edo fire-brigade frogs doing the New Year bamboo-ladder stunts (出初式).
**Twist:** it is a playset. Each frog comes with a bamboo ladder section, and the sections stack into one tall ladder.
Each frog's stunt pose fits a rung: the handstand, the one-leg balance, the "pine tree" splits, the dangle.
**Gimmick:** the playset parts combine on the shelf (the frogs climb as the ladder grows), and pressing the matoi
(standard-bearer) frog spins its matoi. Happi coats with crests, little hachimaki, warty hand-painted skin.
Secret: the old chief frog in a fire helmet with a hose.

## 4. さかさてるてる Sakasa Teru-teru: "Upside-Down Rain Charms" (dark-cute)

**Hook:** teru-teru bōzu, the paper charms children hang for sunny weather, that were hung upside down and turned
into rain-bringers.
**Twist:** they are cute and slightly wrong: a stitched smile, one eye drawn in marker, ink running in the rain,
a cloth that has gone grey and soggy. Each one brings its own weather: drizzle, thunder (with a little drum), hail,
fog, typhoon. Secret: the one that eats rainbows.
**Gimmick:** they are keychains. The ring and ball chain are modelled on, you hang them on a hook or from your bag
and they swing on spring physics as you walk. Press the button and the charm shivers and drips.

## 5. かみのおつかい Kami no Otsukai: "The Gods' Messengers" (mythic)

**Hook:** the animal messengers of Japanese shrines, made as sleek black-lacquer and gold-leaf idols, the way the
boards' Egyptian gods are birds.
**Twist:** each animal carries its shrine's attribute: the Inari fox with the granary key in its mouth, the Kasuga
deer with a sakaki branch on its antlers, the three-legged sun crow, the Hie monkey with its gohei wand, the
Mitsumine wolf in a shimenawa rope. Secret: Benzaiten's white snake coiled round a gold biwa.
**Gimmick:** press the button and the messenger acts: the crow spreads its wings, the fox flicks its tail and the
key turns, the wolf lifts its head and howls. Vermilion torii-step base. Three of them also come as keychains.

## 6. つっぱり動物園 Tsuppari Zoo: "Shōwa Street Delinquents" (street culture with attitude)

**Hook:** an 80s yankī gang of animals: big pompadours, long embroidered coats, school caps worn flat, geta.
**Twist:** they are tough, and they are kind: the bōsōzoku tanuki on a souped-up scooter with a three-tier backrest
is delivering his grandmother's medicine; the banchō bear in the long coat is carrying a stray kitten in it; the
sukeban cat with the long skirt is guarding the class rabbit.
**Gimmick:** press the button: the scooter pops a wheelie, the pompadour bounces, the coat flaps open to show the
kitten. Secret: the gang's old head, an eagle-owl in sunglasses, now the lane's crossing guard.
