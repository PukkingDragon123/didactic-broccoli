# Gacha Garage: the Blender toy line, agent D's three series

Three more original capsule-toy series after the owner's gashapon boards (ref_gacha), each built around a different
kind of toy: a **playset that joins up** (sentō), **pull-back racers** (otenki) and **light-ups** (yomise). Built
straight in the faceted low-poly style (FACET.md: chunky visible facets, about 550–650 tris for a simple figure, up to
900 for a detailed one, 1000 for a secret, a 1024 px
brush-stroke atlas, hand-brushed ink faces). Every figure is its own sculpt with a press gimmick; five figures and a
secret per series. All names and characters are our own; no real product, character or brand.

---

## D1. ゆけむり銭湯 Yukemuri Sentō: "Bathhouse Regulars" (playset that joins up) · id `sento`

**Hook:** the neighbourhood bathhouse of a Shōwa back lane, its regulars all animals.
**Twist:** each one does its bath ritual the way its body was made for it: the crab lathers by blowing bubbles, the
pufferfish puffs up in the cold plunge, the sea otter floats on its back with a basin for a rock, the giraffe can see
over the partition wall, and the boiler man down in the back is a giant salamander (the fire creature of old books).
**Playset:** every figure comes on a section of the bathhouse floor: white mosaic tile with a cobalt band, a drain
groove, dowels on the right and holes on the left. Side by side on a shelf board they click into one long bathhouse,
from the attendant's high seat to the boiler room.
**Gimmick:** press: they do the ritual.

## D2. おてんき便 Otenki-bin: "Weather Express" (pull-back) · id `otenki`

**Hook:** the weather does not just happen: it is delivered, Shōwa style, by animal couriers on cloud scooters.
**Twist:** each cloud is a delivery scooter (a carrier rack, a handlebar with a bell, a little number plate, wheels
hidden in its belly) and each courier carries its weather like a delivery: the thunder calf's drum ring strapped to
the rack, the wind squirrel's furoshiki of wind, the duck's watering can of rain, the ptarmigan's sieve of snow, the
chameleon's rack of paint pots for the rainbow.
**Pull-back:** pull it back on a table and let go: the cloud scoots off (wheels in its belly, `run` clip).
**Gimmick:** press: it delivers.

## D3. しんかい夜店 Shinkai Yomise: "Deep-Sea Night Stalls" (light-up, dark-cute) · id `yomise`

**Hook:** a festival night market at the bottom of the sea.
**Twist:** there is no electricity down there, so every stall is lit by its keeper: the anglerfish's lure is the
fishing stall's lamp, the barreleye's see-through head is the fortune-teller's crystal ball, the firefly squid's arm
tips are the glow-sticks for sale, the isopod carries its paper lanterns on its back. Dark-cute: fangs, odd eyes,
the deep-sea gloom, and they are all trying very hard to make a sale.
**Gimmick:** press: the light comes on and they make the sale (`light` press mode, each with its own colour and blink).

## D4. ヘンシン傘ぶき Henshin Kasabuki: "Umbrella Arms" (mechanical transformation, display stands) · id `kasabuki`

**Hook:** umbrellas that are secretly weapons, each laid on its own black-lacquer sword stand (katana-kake).
**Twist:** the umbrella's own parts become the weapon's: the closed wagasa's canopy is the katana's scabbard, the
bat-umbrella's (kōmori-gasa's) panels fan out into a bat-wing axe blade, the parasol splits into the two limbs of a
bow, the folding umbrella's sections come apart on chains into a three-section staff, the bull's-eye umbrella's canopy
becomes a spearhead, and the secret lantern umbrella opens into a dragon's head.
**Gimmick:** press: the transformation, as a mechanical clip (parts slide, fold, fan and lock with clicks), then it
holds the weapon pose and folds back into the umbrella. At rest it always reads as an umbrella.

contents.
**Twist:** every crab picked the tin that suits it: the blue crab with its paddle legs in a sardine tin, the hermit
crab using a mackerel tin as its shell, the furry mitten crab in a corned-beef tin, the fiddler crab scooping baked
beans with its one huge claw, the coconut crab stealing pineapple rings. Each tin has a proper printed label in the
5x7 pixel font with an original brand.
