# Gacha Garage: the Blender toy line, two food series (F1, F2)

Both are original concepts in the capsule-toy mascot style (CLAUDE.md toy rules): animal × Japanese food fusions,
each with its own gimmick, modelled in Blender in the faceted low-poly line (FACET.md), faces painted with lib/face.py
(species in `face.SPECIES`). No existing series covers food as the figure itself (kanikan has crabs living in tins;
yomise has deep-sea stalls).

---

## F1. すしねどこ Sushi Nedoko: "Sushi Bedtime" (playset with action) · id `nedoko`

**Hook:** every piece of sushi is somebody's bed.
**Twist:** the rice is the futon and the topping is the duvet: each little animal has picked the sushi that suits it
and fallen asleep under it, on a kaiten-zushi plate whose colour is its rarity (the way a conveyor-belt plate's
colour is its price: white, green, red, yellow, black with gold, and a gold plate for the secret).
**Gimmick:** press: the duvet flips back on its hinge, the sleeper sits up, yawns and looks about, flops back and the
duvet falls back over it (the gunkan pair have their own: the otter juggles its roe, the hedgehog uncurls and sniffs).

## F2. くしだんごどうぶつ Kushi Dango Dōbutsu: "Dango Skewer Pals" (spinning, comes apart) · id `dango`

**Hook:** three balls of dango on a bamboo skewer.
**Twist:** each ball is one third of an animal (the top ball the head, the middle the body and arms, the bottom the
feet and tail), and each pal is its own kind of dango, the animal chosen by the sweet's colour and dressing; it stands
in a little wooden skewer block shaped like a geta.
**Gimmick:** press: the balls spin round the skewer one after another (the middle one the other way), like a slot
machine, then the head ball hops up off the tip of the skewer and drops back on.

| 2 | みたらしタヌキ Mitarashi Tanuki | grilled and glazed in soy syrup, drips and scorch marks: tanuki mask, drum belly, ringed tail, a leaf | common |
| 3 | よもぎガエル Mugwort Frog | deep green mugwort dango: eyes on top, a dollop of red bean paste for a hat, webbed hands and feet | uncommon |
| 4 | いそべペンギン Nori-Wrapped Penguin | isobe dango: the nori wrap round the back of each ball is its black coat; beak, flippers, feet | uncommon |
| 5 | きなこグマ Kinako Bear Cub | dusted in roasted soybean flour, dark brown-sugar syrup poured over its head, hugging its tummy | rare |

and a teapot.
