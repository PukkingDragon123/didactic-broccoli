# Gacha Garage: the Blender toy line, agent E's four homage series

The owner: "4 more series too: JoJo characters as animals, and one for other animes." These are **homages, not
copies**: original animal characters that carry the spirit of the source (the bizarre dramatic pose, the outrageous
fashion, the menacing lettering, a ghostly guardian spirit that bursts out behind its owner; the classic anime
genres), never a specific character's design, name, power name or catchphrase, and no logos. The lettering on the
press is the game's own ゴゴゴ sound (src/jojo/kana.js), a generic manga sound effect. Built straight in the faceted
low-poly style (FACET.md: ~550–650 tris simple, up to 900 detailed, 1000 for a secret, 1024 px brush-stroke atlas,
hand-brushed ink faces). Every figure is its own sculpt with a press gimmick; five figures and a secret per series
(six and a secret for the anime club). The poses are the point: each figure is a strong full-body silhouette.

Shared builder: `art/blender/toys/sabaku/_bz.py` (an anthropomorphic animal from lofts and tubes in a posed skeleton:
torso, coat, limbs, mitt hands, shoes, species heads, the clear-tint spirit, the series base), used by all four.

The spirits: a second sculpt in the `glass` material (translucent, tinted by its paint). At rest it rides small on
its owner's back or shoulder (the charm of the trope: it is always with you); on the press it swells up behind
them to full size and does its move.
