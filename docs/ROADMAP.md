# Gacha Garage roadmap

The owner's wishlist for the big rework, grouped by area. Status: **in progress** (an agent is on it now),
**planned** (next phases), **adapted** (done differently, reason noted).

## Phase 1 — foundations (in progress)
- Feel: no white hover highlight anywhere; every particle is a 2D pixel-art sprite; blocky first-person arms
  (old hand model) that are connected and flexible; empty-hand click = slap; drop any item, key included;
  6 inventory slots.
- The cat: fat, fluffy, expressive voxel calico you can pet; milk pouring physics.
- Machines and capsules remade as polished voxel art (based on real Bandai / Takara Tomy / Kitan Club gashapon);
  restocking in tiny steps (open the delivery box, insert capsules one by one, close and lock); coins collect in
  the machine's coin box and you collect them physically; a price plate on each machine; machine upgrades removed.
- Capsule shells stay in your hand after opening: drop them, bin them or sweep them.
- Higher-resolution figures; interactive toys (dart gun, squishy, wind-up robot...); an off-brand pop-culture
  parody series; acrylic stands and packaging so rare figures can be displayed and sold.
- The computer: sit down and zoom in, a realistic desktop OS on the monitor itself, browser, messenger, mail with
  spam, bank (bills, loans), calendar, codex, phone dialer (110 police...), timed-choice prompts, achievements,
  online promotion.
- Simulation: game clock with day/night, calendar with special days (rent day, holidays), bills (rent,
  electricity, water) and loans, player level that unlocks things, price vs recommended price with complaints,
  money only from coin boxes and sales, much rarer odds, an empty start where you buy your first machine,
  faster early progression and more customers, an end-of-day summary that zooms out over the sunset.

## Phase 2 — commerce, people, life (planned)
- Commerce: an "Off-Hobby" second-hand marketplace (random items with grades, flaws and funny variants such as
  stickers and doodles), supplier sites, luck charms category (traditional Japanese items), thrift clothes,
  talking to sellers and haggling (timed choices), deliveries by a "FREE CANDY" van whose old driver remembers you
  and hands you candy, selling with custom price tags, grading and bidding; physical cash vs bank, an ATM, a
  piggy bank; cardboard boxes for storage; furniture and props you buy that actually do something (fewer random
  props); a robot vacuum; building / placement with rotation.
- People: cinematic chats with floating text and choices instead of speech bubbles; real voice lines (browser
  text-to-speech) and talking to yourself; NPCs with names who remember you and give quests; customers who ask
  questions, haggle and complain; knocking on doors; neighbours who visit; quests over the messenger; funny
  locals (a prankster, a pigeon lover, a sorcerer, a straw-hat pirate parody, a guy camping in a bush you can
  confront and slap); yokai NPCs and a yokai collection; the bizarre-adventure cast and the masked old man at
  the corner shop; a biologist who teaches plant care.
- Life: a better intro (you arrive in an old van, Grandpa's notes) and an opening made of many small steps
  (clean, take out trash, hang the OPEN sign...); side hustles before the shop pays (walking a neighbour's
  dog, washing cars and windows, sweeping streets, cashier shifts, washing dishes); trash: customers bin their
  shells, you take the bags to the dumpster, a garbage truck collects; energy you refill with food (corner shop,
  vending machine, ramen) or rest; a bedroom (make the bed, sleep, decorate, sofa and furniture); plants to grow
  and water; a ramen shop with a physical menu, drinks, gyoza, eating with friends; a vending machine with lots
  of products you can inspect, drinks with pros and cons (you can get drunk); interactive phones and gumball
  machines; cat chases pigeons, pigeons poop.
- World: neighbours' houses much better; the park, the mangaka's villa and the steel tower; a more faithful
  "don't look back" alley.

## Phase 3 — growth, multiplayer, release (planned)
- Bigger home (more rooms), buy the empty building next door, grow the shop up to a mall with an escalator and
  an elevator.
- Employees from a job site you unlock ("Inspired"): interviews, procedural funny candidates (a kappa...),
  assigning work, talking to them.
- Multiplayer: the host owns the world, friends sleep on cardboard; shared piggy-bank wallet; slapping,
  stealing and calling the police (110) on friends, with fines for pranks.
- Cosmetics: achievements and quests that reward cosmetics, cosmetics on the web shop, thrift clothes.
- Minimal traditional UI: more things happen in the world (signs, papers, screens) instead of panels.
- Performance pass (the game should never lag), then the Steam page: capsule art, description, four screenshots.

## Adapted
- Pinterest references: not reachable from the build machine and the designs belong to their makers; capsule
  and machine designs are based on real gashapon from general knowledge instead.
- Album covers as shirt prints: copyrighted; thrift shirts use pixelated CC-licensed photos (Google Open Images)
  and original pixel art instead.
- The biologist character has realistic adult proportions, with hair and clothes swaying; no chest physics.
- The guy hiding in the bush is just a weird local camping out and spying on the street; no kids involved.
