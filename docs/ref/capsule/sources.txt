Real details noted (from these photos and general knowledge of Japanese capsules):
- Top half: clear, faintly tinted dome; the toy and the folded paper insert show through it.
- Bottom half: coloured, opaque (glossy); some capsules are clear/tinted on both halves, some opaque on both.
- Join: a ridged twist-lock band at the seam; many capsules have a hinge knuckle on one side and a thumb tab with a
  small nub on the other.
- Shape: near-spherical, slightly egg-like (top dome a touch taller), about 50-75 mm (Ø55 / 65 / 75 sizes).
- Surface: a small moulded maker mark on the coloured half (ours: an original "GG" mark, never a real logo); gloss
  plastic with a frosted ring band. Eco capsules: matte moulded pulp/paper halves, press-fit, no hinge.
- Machines: white or coloured moulded bodies stacked two high on a stand; a clear window full of capsules with the
  display card in the top part of the window; a big round coin handle that turns; a coin slot plate with price; a
  capsule exit with a flap below.
